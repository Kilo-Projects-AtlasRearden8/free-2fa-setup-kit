2FA Backup Plan

Make a plan before you lose access • Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Make a plan before you lose access. If your phone is lost, stolen, or broken — or you lose your authenticator app — this plan gets you back into your accounts.

Before you’re locked out (do this now)

  • ☐ Recovery codes saved for every account with 2FA
  • ☐ Recovery codes printed and stored offline
  • ☐ A second device can generate codes (tablet, old phone, secondary authenticator app)
  • ☐ A security key is registered (optional, but an excellent backup)
  • ☐ Recovery email and phone number are current on every account
  • ☐ Account recovery options (trusted contacts, recovery keys) are set up where offered
  • ☐ You know each account’s account-recovery page URL

If you lose your phone or authenticator app

  1. Stay calm. Recovery codes get you in — that’s what they’re for.
  2. Sign in with a recovery code on a trusted computer or borrowed device.
  3. Revoke the old device if the service lets you remove lost devices from trusted sessions.
  4. Set up 2FA again on a new phone or device, using the authenticator app and setup key or QR code.
  5. Generate new recovery codes and print them; destroy or cross out old printed copies.
  6. Update this backup plan with any changes.

Quarterly review (set a reminder)

  • ☐ Recovery codes still match what the services issued
  • ☐ Printed copies still exist and are readable
  • ☐ Recovery email and phone number are current
  • ☐ Secondary device still generates codes
  • ☐ No printed codes left where others could find them
