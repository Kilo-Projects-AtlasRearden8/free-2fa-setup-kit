How to use

  1. Open the Markdown files in any text editor, or open the HTML files in any web browser.
  2. Print the HTML files (File → Print). They are styled for clean printing with no trackers, headers, or footers.
  3. Fill in the worksheets by hand. Keep printed copies somewhere safe and private.

Privacy

This kit is privacy-first and local-first:

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or email addresses required
  • No trackers, analytics, or third-party scripts
  • Nothing is sent to a server — everything stays on your device or on paper
  • Recovery codes can bypass 2FA. Store printed codes offline, never in email or unencrypted cloud notes
