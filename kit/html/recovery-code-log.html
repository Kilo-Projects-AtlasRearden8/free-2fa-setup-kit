Warning: Recovery codes let you into your account if you lose your phone or cannot use your authenticator app. They bypass 2FA, so anyone who has them can get into your account. Print this log and keep the paper somewhere safe. Do not save recovery codes in email, unencrypted notes, or photos on your phone.

Safety reminders

  • Recovery codes are single-use on most services. Cross each one off after you use it.
  • If you use a code, generate a new set of recovery codes in the service’s security settings and update this log.
  • Never share a recovery code with anyone — not even someone claiming to be support.
  • If you think your codes were seen or stolen, sign in, invalidate the old codes, and generate new ones immediately.
  • Review this log every few months so the locations and codes still match reality.
