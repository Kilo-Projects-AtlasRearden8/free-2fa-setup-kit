# Two-Factor Authentication Setup Worksheet

Use this sheet to walk through turning on two-factor authentication (2FA) for one account. Print it and fill it in by hand. One worksheet per account.

Privacy note: This sheet asks for account details. Keep it somewhere safe and private. Do not store passwords or recovery codes in unencrypted email or notes.

## Before you start

- [ ] You can sign in to the account right now
- [ ] You have a phone or device for the authenticator app
- [ ] You have a pen and somewhere safe to write down recovery codes
- [ ] You know where the account's security or sign-in settings live

## Step-by-step instructions

1. **Sign in to your account** on the service's website or app.
2. **Go to security settings.** Look for "Security," "Sign-in & security," "Two-factor authentication," "2FA," or "MFA."
3. **Turn on two-factor authentication.** Choose "Authenticator app" when asked for the method (it is safer than SMS).
4. **Add the account to your authenticator app.** Scan the QR code on the screen, or type the setup key shown under "Can't scan?" into your app manually.
   - If you write down the manual setup key, treat it like a password — anyone with it can generate your codes.
5. **Verify it works.** Your app will show a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds. Enter the current code on the service's website to finish setup.
6. **Save your recovery codes.** The service will show one-time recovery (backup) codes. Write them down or print them. They let you into your account if you lose your phone.
7. **Test it.** Sign out, then sign back in using a code from your authenticator app.
8. **Mark it complete.** Fill in the account information table above and file this worksheet with your records.
