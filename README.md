# Free 2FA Setup Kit

A free, printable, privacy-first kit for setting up two-factor authentication (2FA) on your online accounts. No account needed, nothing to install, no tracking. Print the worksheets and fill them in by hand.

## What's inside

| File | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| `kit/setup-worksheet.md` / `.html` | Walk through enabling 2FA on any account, step by step |
| `kit/recovery-code-log.md` / `.html` | Record recovery codes and where they are stored |
| `kit/authenticator-comparison.md` / `.html` | Compare authenticator apps side by side |
| `kit/backup-plan.md` / `.html` | Plan for lost phones, broken devices, and account recovery |

## How to use

1. Open the Markdown files in any text editor, or open the HTML files in any web browser.
2. Print the HTML files (File → Print). They are styled for clean printing with no trackers, headers, or footers.
3. Fill in the worksheets by hand. Keep printed copies somewhere safe and private.

## Privacy

This kit is privacy-first and local-first:

- No accounts, sign-ups, or email addresses required
- No trackers, analytics, or third-party scripts
- Nothing is sent to a server — everything stays on your device or on paper
- Recovery codes can bypass 2FA. Store printed codes offline, never in email or unencrypted cloud notes
